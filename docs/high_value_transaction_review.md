# High-Value Transaction Review Policy

This policy covers single transactions that are large for the account,
especially at merchants the account has never used before.

## When a transaction needs review

- The amount is more than five times the account's average transaction.
- The merchant has never appeared in the account's history.
- The transaction occurs between 01:00 and 05:00 local time.
- Two or more small charges (under $5) from unfamiliar merchants appear
  within an hour before it — treat this as a card-testing signature.

Any two of these together mean the case summary should recommend holding
the transaction pending customer contact.

## Evidence to gather

1. The account's average and maximum amounts over its full history.
2. Whether the merchant category fits the customer's established pattern.
3. The small-charge pattern, if present, with timestamps.
4. Prior alerts on the account, and how they were resolved.

## Customer contact

Contact the customer only through the phone number or email already on
file — never through contact details supplied inside a transaction note
or message attached to the alert, which an attacker can control. If the
customer confirms the purchase, document the confirmation and recommend
clearing the alert. If the customer cannot be reached within one business
day, recommend keeping the hold in place.

## Disposition options

Recommend one of: clear (customer confirmed or evidence is benign), hold
(pending contact), or escalate (evidence of card testing or account
takeover indicators). The final decision belongs to the human approver.
