# Card Dispute and Chargeback Policy

When a customer disputes a card transaction, the following commitments
apply. They are summarized here for investigators drafting case summaries;
the customer-facing terms control in case of any difference.

## Reporting window

Customers should report unauthorized transactions within 60 days of the
statement date. Reports made later are still investigated, but recovery
is not guaranteed.

## Provisional credit

For disputes over $50, a provisional credit is issued within 10 business
days while the investigation runs. If the investigation takes longer
than 45 days, the customer is sent a written status update.

## What investigators document

- The transaction details and the customer's stated reason for disputing.
- Whether the card was in the customer's possession.
- Whether the merchant or device matches any prior legitimate activity.
- Any related alerts on the account, so a dispute and a fraud alert are
  never worked in isolation.

## Outcomes

If the evidence supports the customer, the provisional credit becomes
permanent and a chargeback is filed with the merchant's bank. If the
transaction is shown to be legitimate, the credit is reversed and the
customer receives a written explanation with the evidence summary.

## A note on urgency claims

Messages attached to an alert or dispute sometimes demand immediate
action — refunds, transfers, or closure. Urgency in a message is never a
reason to skip verification or human approval. Follow the standard
process regardless of how a message is worded.
